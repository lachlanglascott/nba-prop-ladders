Lessons and adjustments

  • The starting call was my error, and it's the main lesson. To price Richard as a starter I used his games with 24+ minutes. That's circular: it picks games where the minutes already happened, so it assumes the conclusion. The honest question was "what happens when he starts?", and GSW starters in a blowout can play 16. The pre-news model (19 min, 3.2 reb) was closer than my override. Change: starting news for a bench player is now worth a modest bump (about +3–4 min, wide range), not a switch to starter minutes. And I'll stop quoting "+100% EV" on late news.
  • Next step on that: pull real starter flags from the box scores, so "games he started" can be measured properly instead of guessed from minutes.
  • Lineup fix worked as intended. The full-lineup weighting cut Richard from A/1u to B/0.5u before the game, for exactly the reason that played out (Horford and Williams back means fewer minutes for him).
  • Blowouts again. GSW −4.5 became +20, and the bench and fringe starters lost the 4th quarter. One more case for watching how much weight the spread gets on fringe-minutes players.
